## Users

- **Primary: commercial staff and managers at IT, office-equipment (bureautique) and B2B e-commerce resellers in France.** They already own the client relationship (SMB/ETI accounts asking "we want to equip our meeting room") but have no in-house audiovisual expertise. Their job: turn that request into a correct, compatible, in-stock, profitable quote fast, without hiring an AV expert and without the risk of a technical incompatibility discovered mid-project.
- **Secondary audiences (ecosystem partners):** AV brands/manufacturers (Logitech, HP Poly, Jabra, Yealink, Shure, Microsoft, Samsung, Zoom), distributors/wholesalers (Ingram Micro, TD Synnex, Also, Alliance Com), and installers/technical service providers. Named brands and wholesalers are market players the catalogue targets, not confirmed signed partners.

## Product Purpose

La Ruche by InovElite is a B2B SaaS that digitises and industrialises the sales cycle of professional audiovisual (videoconferencing / meeting-room) solutions, from needs qualification to a priced quote. A non-expert reseller salesperson describes the client's room and gets, in minutes, a compatible multi-brand hardware recommendation available at partner wholesalers, plus installation/maintenance services, a priced quote, product sheets and a room synoptic, ready to send. Today the same path takes days or weeks of calls to brands, wholesalers and installers, and often ends with the project abandoned.

Success: resellers open a new growth line (AV) without recruiting, sell faster with controlled margins, and stop refusing projects.

## Positioning

- The only tool built for the **non-expert IT reseller**: multi-brand, connected to wholesaler prices/stock/lead times, with quote generation, built-in gamified training, and escalation to a human expert.
- Between AV design software built for expert integrators (D-Tools, XTEN-AV, Jetbuilt) and generic CPQs (Visiativ, Simple CPQ, Clarendis), and versus mono-brand, slow manufacturer/wholesaler pre-sales or the Excel-and-phone status quo (~90% of resellers).
- A **connector, not a competitor**: hardware keeps its usual circuit (brands → wholesalers → resellers → end clients, installers deploy). La Ruche circulates the missing information. It does not capture the reseller's margin. "Le client reste le vôtre."
- Backed by InovElite's existing manual business (outsourced AV pre-sales, project management through installation and maintenance), not a theoretical bet.
- Pricing principle: "price on value delivered, not on cost to serve", made fully traceable and verifiable by the client.

## Operating Context

Reseller user journey, 4 steps:
1. **Qualification**: guided questionnaire (room size, uses, number of participants, existing equipment). No technical question.
2. **Configuration**: coherent multi-brand recommendation (camera, audio, display, room controller) checked for compatibility, availability at wholesalers and fit to use, plus installation/maintenance, in minutes.
3. **Automatic quote**: priced recommendation, product sheets, detailed quote, room synoptic, margin visible to the salesperson.
4. **Escalation if needed**: out-of-standard projects go via a traced ticket to a dedicated InovElite expert. The client stays the reseller's.

Services marketplace: an installer from the partner network matched to the project's area. InovElite runs the job end to end.

Trust mechanics around billing: a configuration = one quote generated and validated (timestamped, attributed, frozen, viewable in a real-time dashboard with exportable history). 80% quota alert with upgrade simulation, annual audit right, calculation logic published in the terms, monthly revenue report attached to each invoice. Success fee only when a traced expert escalation (≥30 min) AND a quote over €20,000 excl. VAT are both met (3% of the quote).

## Product Principles

1. **The reseller is the hero.** The client relationship and the margin stay theirs; La Ruche makes them the expert.
2. **Minutes, not weeks.** Every claim and interaction should prove speed and simplicity, with no AV jargon required.
3. **Connect, don't replace.** Brands, wholesalers, installers and InovElite each keep their role; the information finally circulates.
4. **Verifiable by design.** Pricing, quotas, success fees and quotes are traceable, timestamped and visible to the client. Transparency is a feature.
5. **Honest about stage.** Pre-launch, pilot-driven, built on a proven manual practice. Credibility over hype.
